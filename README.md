# Peloton Business and Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project combines business process analysis and data analytics to evaluate the performance of Peloton Interactive Inc. The study investigates strategic challenges, organisational performance, stakeholder relationships, and operational analytics using business analysis frameworks and Power BI dashboards.

---

## Business Analysis Component

The project evaluates:

- Peloton's business model
- Strategic objectives
- Revenue performance
- Stakeholder relationships
- Business process effectiveness
- Competitive position

The analysis incorporates:

- SWOT Analysis
- Stakeholder Mapping
- Needs Analysis
- Business Model Canvas
- Business Process Evaluation

---

## SWOT Analysis

The SWOT analysis identified several strengths, including Peloton's integrated product ecosystem, strong brand identity, and engaged customer community.

Challenges identified include:

- High product costs
- Dependence on subscription revenue
- Increasing market competition
- Supply chain and operational challenges

---

## Stakeholder Analysis

A Power-Interest Grid was developed to categorise stakeholders into:

- Manage Closely
- Keep Satisfied
- Keep Informed
- Monitor

This framework helped identify key decision-makers, influence groups, and stakeholder priorities within the Peloton ecosystem.

---

## Business Analytics Component

The project analysed Peloton workout data using Power BI and Python.

### Dataset Characteristics

- 3,737 workout records
- 21 variables
- Multiple years of activity data

---

## Key Findings

The analysis revealed:

- Total workout output of **153,099**
- Total calories burned of **402,740**
- **2022** recorded the highest concentration of activity
- Strong positive relationship between calories burned and workout output
- Hiking and Walk + Run activities generated significant output
- Instructor **Kirsten Ferguson** emerged as a major contributor to workout engagement
- Sunday recorded the highest workout activity levels
- Peak workout periods occurred between **7:00 AM–10:00 AM** and **6:00 PM–7:00 PM**
- On-demand workouts generated higher activity levels and calorie expenditure than live workouts
